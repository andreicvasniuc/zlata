Before vs. After Comparison

Every design decision directly targets luxury brand elevation and bridal consultation conversions.

UX / UI Element Current Site (AngularJS/Bootstrap 3 Port) Modernized Proposal (Couture Standard)
Header & Legibility Dark grey links over dark hero photo without background overlay; unreadable until scrolled. Subtle dark top gradient scrim + crisp white text, transitioning to frosted glass on scroll.
Color Palette Electric cyan blue (#28bbf8 / #4dc2eb) reminiscent of a 2012 tech dashboard. Champagne gold (#C5A880), warm alabaster, soft charcoal, and delicate stone borders.
Typography Single sans-serif font (Lato) with standard body weights. High-fashion serif (Cormorant Garamond / Playfair) paired with geometric sans (Plus Jakarta Sans).
Product / Collection Names Completely hidden (opacity: 0) until desktop hover; invisible on mobile/touch screens. Always visible below each card with silhouette subtitle, color dots, and wishlist heart icon.
Product Detail & Conversion Hardcoded margin-left: 824px, clipped text, no inquiry button, no booking, no wishlist. Sticky 2-column gallery + details, "Inquire About This Gown", "Book a Fitting", and size guide.
Catalog Browsing Repeated blurry generic stock photo on every page; zero filter or search options. Faceted filter pills (Silhouette, Neckline, Sleeves, Season) + sorting and 2/3 column layout toggle.
Mobile Navigation Pitch black 70%-width drawer, no logo, no close button, no instant messengers. Warm white slide-over drawer with brand logo, accordion categories, WhatsApp/Telegram buttons.
Footer Empty white box with 5 floating social circles (including VK) and copyright. 4-column editorial footer: Atelier story, Collections, Customer Service & Retailers, Newsletter signup.
